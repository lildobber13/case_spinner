You're the consultants. Spin up a random industry, a client with a headache, and a few awkward constraints. Then turn it into a build brief and ship a working prototype with the AI coding assistant of your choice. No API keys. No installs. Your solution opens in a browser.

Say it in one sentence

If you can't say it clearly, your coding assistant will have to guess.

What does your user get to do? Start with a verb.
